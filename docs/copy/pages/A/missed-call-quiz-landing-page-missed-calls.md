Missed calls are the quietest leak in an HVAC shop.
You can't see a missed call the way you see a broken part. There's no invoice for it. The homeowner calls someone else, and you never know the job was there.
Think about when calls come in. The first hot week of summer. The first cold night of fall. Those are the days your crew is busiest. Those are the days missed calls hurt most.
A homeowner with no AC or no heat doesn't wait long. They want a person, or at least a fast reply. If they get voicemail, they may hang up and call the next shop.
Web leads are the same. A form sits in an inbox for hours. By the time you call back, they booked someone else.
It's frustrating. You do good work. You can't be on a job and on the phone at once.
A good shop should not lose a job to a voicemail.
Small numbers add up fast.
Here's a simple example. Say you miss 5 calls a week. Say 2 of them were real jobs worth $400 each. That's $800 a week, or more than $3,000 a month.
Now think about install calls. One missed new system call can be worth far more than a repair. And a customer who stays with you for years is worth more than any single job.
The quiz runs this math with your own numbers.
If no one picks up, the next shop gets the job, the review and the customer for life.
